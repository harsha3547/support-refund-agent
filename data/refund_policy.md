# Refund policy (Northwind Outfitters, fictional store)

1. **Window:** items can be refunded within 30 days of delivery.
2. **Damaged or wrong items:** refundable within 60 days of delivery, regardless of the 30-day window.
3. **Not refundable:** gift cards and items marked final sale, unless they arrived damaged or wrong.
4. **Undelivered orders:** an order still in transit cannot be refunded; offer to track it instead.
5. **Amount:** a refund can never exceed what the customer paid for the item, minus anything already refunded for it.
6. **Approval:** every refund and every order status change must be approved by a support lead before it is issued.
